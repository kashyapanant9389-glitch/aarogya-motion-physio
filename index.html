Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you get in touch.

How do I book a physiotherapy session?

Fill in the appointment request form or use the WhatsApp button. The clinic can then discuss availability and next steps with you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

If relevant, bring previous reports, scans or your clinician's advice, along with comfortable clothing that allows movement.

What are the consultation fees and timings?

Please contact the clinic directly for current fees, operating hours and appointment availability.

Will treatment be the same for everyone?

No two people have exactly the same needs. Your plan should be discussed individually after the physiotherapist understands your situation.
